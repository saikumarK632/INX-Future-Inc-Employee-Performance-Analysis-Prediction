# Requirement – INX Future Inc Employee Performance Project

**Project code:** 10281 (International Association of Business Analytics Certification)
**Company:** INX Future Inc, a data analytics and automation solutions provider

## Business situation
Employee performance has declined in recent years. Escalations on service delivery have increased and client satisfaction has fallen by 8 percentage points. The chief executive officer, Mr. Brain, does not want to penalise low performers blindly, because that could hurt the morale of all employees and the company's reputation as a top employer. He wants the **root causes** of the problem and **clear indicators** of non performing employees, so that any action can be targeted.

## Insights requested
1. Department wise performance
2. The top three factors affecting employee performance
3. A trained model that predicts employee performance from the factors (to be used in hiring)
4. Recommendations to improve employee performance

## Data provided
`INX_Future_Inc_Employee_Performance_CDS_Project2_Data_V1_8.xls`
- Sheet 1: 1,200 employees and 28 columns (26 factors, the employee number and the performance rating)
- Sheet 2: definitions of the coded scales (education, satisfaction, work life balance, performance rating)
- Target column: `PerformanceRating` (2 Good, 3 Excellent, 4 Outstanding)
